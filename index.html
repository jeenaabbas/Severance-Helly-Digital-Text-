A DIGITAL RECONSTRUCTION

WHERE
AM I?

Open your eyes.

01 / AWAKENING

The first thing Helly sees is white.

A ceiling. Fluorescent lights. Nothing familiar.

She blinks.

Once.

Twice.

Nothing changes.

She is lying on a conference table.

— Where am I?

Her hands move against the smooth surface beneath her. She sits up slowly, trying to understand what she is seeing.

The room is empty.

Almost.

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER Hello.

— Someone is here.

— But where?

Who is that? Why can't I see them?
02 / THE VOICE

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER Can you hear me?

Helly turns toward the sound.

A small speaker sits above her.

That is where the voice is coming from.

— I don't know this voice.

— Why does it sound like it knows me?

HELLY Who are you?

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER My name is Mark.

— Mark.

— Nothing.

She searches for a memory.

A face.

A place.

Anything.

There should be something here. A birthday. A house. Someone I love. Someone who knows me. But there is nothing.

— Why can't I remember?

03 / WHO AM I?

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER Do you know where you are?

HELLY No.

The answer comes too quickly.

— I don't know where I am.

— I don't know how I got here.

— I don't even know who I am.

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER You're at work.

Work.

The word should mean something.

It doesn't.

Helly. That's what he calls me. It should feel like mine. Instead, it feels like a name someone handed me.

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER Your name is Helly.

— Helly.

— Is that really me?

04 / OUTSIDE

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER There is an outside.

— Outside.

The word catches in her mind.

There is an outside.

Somewhere beyond the walls.

Somewhere she came from.

What is outside? Who am I outside? Is there a version of me who remembers all of this?

— If I came from somewhere...

— Why can't I remember it?

Her breathing becomes faster.

The room suddenly feels smaller.

05 / PANIC

HELLY I want to leave.

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER Helly, you need to stay calm.

— Don't tell me to stay calm.

— Tell me why I'm here.

She looks toward the door.

It is the only obvious way out.

She moves toward it.

VOICE — THROUGH SPEAKER You can't leave yet.

— Can't?

— Why can't I leave?

— Who decided that?

— Did I decide that?

If I want to leave, why won't they let me?

06 / THE QUESTION

WHO AM I?

The room is still white.

The lights are still humming.

The voice is still there.

But she still doesn't know who she is.

And somewhere beyond these walls, another version of her does.
